Refund Policy

DRAFT of 2026-08-26  ·  Effective date: [not yet in effect]  ·  Contact: support@sabrtechnologies.com

Every SI comes with a free 24-hour trial of the same brain and the same installer as the paid product — meet one before you buy one. Because you can try the real thing first, our paid refund window is simple and short.

1.The window

  • First Care payment: full refund of your first payment if you request it within [14] days of purchase. No forms, no interrogation — tell us it didn’t fit.
  • Renewal payments: renewals are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and your plan simply ends at the close of the paid period. If a renewal charged within [72 hours] before you cancelled, ask and we will refund that renewal.
  • Installation fee: the one-time installation fee is fully refundable until hands-on install work begins. Once installation has started, it is refundable [on a pro-rata basis for work not yet performed — Dean to confirm]. The Care part that follows installation uses the normal [14]-day window.
  • Custom team deployments are governed by their own signed agreement, not this policy.
  • Trials carry no Sabr charge; there is nothing to refund.
  • Hardware: Sabr does not sell or resell hardware, so there is no Sabr hardware purchase to refund. Returns for a machine are between you and the seller.

2.How to request one

  1. Email support@sabrtechnologies.com from the email address you purchased with, with the subject “Refund”.
  2. Include your Stripe receipt number (in your receipt email) or the approximate purchase date. That is all we need.
  3. We confirm and issue the refund through Stripe to your original payment method. We aim to issue within [5] business days of your request; once issued, Stripe typically posts it to your card in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Refunds are issued in the currency and to the payment method of the original charge. Where Stripe does not return its processing fee to us, we still refund you the full amount you paid.

3.What a refund does to your SI

A refund revokes the SI’s license token. When your refund is issued, the license token from that purchase is revoked in our licensing system. Your installed SI checks in daily; on its next check-in it will learn the license is no longer active and will deactivate on your machine. This is automatic. Any data the SI created on your machine remains yours and remains on your machine.

The same applies to chargebacks and payment disputes: a disputed or reversed charge suspends the token immediately. If a dispute was a mistake (they happen), email us — reinstating a wrongly-suspended license is quick.

4.Fair use of this policy

  • One first-purchase refund per customer. The refund window is for honest “this isn’t for me” decisions, not for cycling licenses.
  • We may decline a refund where the license terms were materially breached (for example, attempts to extract or redistribute the brain payload) — see the Terms & EULA.
  • Nothing here limits any non-waivable rights you have under the consumer law of your place of residence.

5.Questions

Anything unclear, or anything gone sideways with a charge — write to support@sabrtechnologies.com and a human (usually the founder) will sort it out.
